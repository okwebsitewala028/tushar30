# **App Name**: ApexAccountants

## Core Features:

- Structured Content Display: Render a multi-page website including a compliant Header, static Home, About Us, Careers, and Contact sections, along with a compliant Footer with copyright and ICAI notes.
- ICAI-Compliant Services Gateway: Implement a mechanism to display firm services (e.g., Audit, Taxation) only upon an explicit user click, adhering strictly to ICAI guidelines (Clause 3.3) for compliance.
- Partner Profile Cards: Present detailed profiles for firm partners, including their name, qualification year, contact information, and professional photograph in a dedicated 'Partners' section.
- Educational Content Section: Provide a section for publishing and displaying informative articles, blogs, or educational content relevant to the CA profession and financial regulations.
- Basic Compliance Inquiry Tool: An AI-powered tool to offer generalized, educational responses to frequently asked questions about basic ICAI compliance or common tax concepts, always with a clear disclaimer regarding legal advice.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated, deep blue (#1F5DCC) conveying trust, professionalism, and clarity. This shade will anchor the brand identity without being overly assertive.
- Background color: A very light, almost off-white grey-blue (#EEF1F4), derived from the primary hue but heavily desaturated to create an open, clean, and unobtrusive canvas for content.
- Accent color: A muted purple (#5A38C9) that offers a complementary yet distinctive highlight, providing visual interest and differentiating calls to action from the primary color scheme without clashing.
- Headline font: 'Alegreya' (serif), chosen for its elegant, intellectual, and contemporary feel, lending authority and gravitas to headings. Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), for its modern, neutral, and highly readable qualities, ensuring clarity and professionalism for all textual content.
- Use minimalist, clean line icons for navigation and functional elements, maintaining a professional and unambiguous visual language across the site. Avoid decorative or overly abstract designs.
- A well-defined, structured grid layout emphasizing ample white space to enhance readability and a professional, uncluttered appearance. Clearly segment content into distinct, accessible sections (header, main content areas, footer).
- Subtle and purposeful animations, such as smooth transitions on navigation hovers and content reveals, particularly for the ICAI-compliant Services section to clearly indicate user interaction triggering content display.
